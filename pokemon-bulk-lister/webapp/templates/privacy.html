Privacy policy

Pokémon Bulk Lister is run by DTF Services LLC, St. Augustine, Florida. This policy took effect on 18 November 2026.

What the app is for

The app is used by the operator to list the operator's own Pokémon cards for sale on eBay and other marketplaces. The app identifies cards from photos, prices the cards, and creates and updates the operator's listings.

What the app stores

  • The operator's eBay OAuth tokens, so the app can call eBay's Sell APIs on the operator's behalf (creating, updating and reading the operator's own listings and orders). The tokens are encrypted at rest and are never shown to anyone or sent anywhere except eBay.
  • The operator's card photos, card details, prices and listing records.

The app does not store eBay buyers' personal data. Sales read back from eBay are recorded against the card that sold, without buyer names, addresses or contact details.

Sharing

DTF Services LLC does not sell user data and does not share user data with anyone. Data goes to a marketplace only when the operator lists or updates a card there.

eBay account deletion

The app is subscribed to eBay's marketplace account deletion notifications at /api/ebay/deletion. When eBay reports that a user has asked for data deletion, the app removes anything held about that user. The app holds no data keyed to eBay buyers or other eBay users, so in practice there is nothing to remove, and the notice is acknowledged.
